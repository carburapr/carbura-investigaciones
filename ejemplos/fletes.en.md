Moving a forty-foot container along the eight main routes of world trade costs
4,339 dollars, according to Drewry's index of 13 August. So far this year the
price is up 104.7%, and since the floor of late February, 138.5%.

For an island that [imports between 85% and 90% of what it
eats](/temas/alimentos-importados), a freight index is not shipping news. It is
the price of the weekly shop a few months from now.

The lag is the part that fools people. The freight is paid when the box leaves,
and it reaches the shelf weeks or months later, mixed in with everything else.
By the time the increase shows up in the supermarket, the index has been
reporting it for a while.
