In 2025, 42,260 million dollars of pharmaceutical products left Puerto Rico:
67.69% of everything the island exported. Medical devices added 5,674 million
and food 3,324.

An export economy concentrated like that reads two ways and both are true. It is
an enviable position, because these are products of enormous value per kilo and
whoever makes them does not move easily. And it is an enormous exposure, because
the decisions that move that figure are taken in corporate boardrooms and tax
policy committees, not on the island.

The contrast with [what gets eaten](/temas/alimentos-importados) is the
interesting part. The island exports two thirds in medicine and imports nine
tenths of its food. They are two flows passing through the same docks in
opposite directions, and only one of them comes up when somebody talks about the
economy.
